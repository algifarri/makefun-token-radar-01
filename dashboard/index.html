How Compass-1 prices a launch

implied = TVL × category multiple × momentum × maturity × breadth

Category multiple

The median market-cap / TVL ratio of the snapshot's own trading protocols in the same category (only TVL ≥ $1M counts), clamped to 0.02–3. A category with fewer than 2 trading peers uses the global multiple instead: the median of all the category medians.

Momentum · maturity · breadth

Momentum is 1 + clamped 7-day change (−40%…+80%) × 0.35. Maturity is 0.75 + min(age, 4 years) / 4 years × 0.5; an unknown listing date counts as 365 days. Breadth adds 3% per extra chain, up to six.

Conviction 0–100

TVL on a log scale (40), momentum (20), maturity (20), breadth (10) and evidence (10): the evidence leg scores how many trading peers the multiple rests on — 10+ peers earn 10, 5–9 earn 8, 3–4 earn 6, exactly 2 earn 4, and the global fallback earns 2.
